> A source-supported answer can still present a narrower and more settled account than the evidence available to the system.

## 00 / First pass

AI becomes the **first pass** when retrieval, ranking, and synthesis assemble the first workable account of a documentary field before a user compares its sources. The study follows this process through four connected stages: source availability, selection, structured synthesis, and category assignment.

The central question is whether a pipeline can preserve source traceability while losing competing evidence, unresolved disagreement, or a materially new distinction.

## 01 / Evidence design

The study joins four evidence modules with different inferential roles:

- two controlled donor-derived corpora with neutralised controls for stage-level mechanism tests;
- a public-document-derived, semi-synthetic automated-hiring anchor with 14 dated source cards, 160 rendered documents, 32 novelty documents, and 32 queries;
- backend and domain-sensitivity checks across Qwen, Gemma, DeepSeek, Gemini, and a platform-AI transfer anchor;
- an exploratory human-reading module with 17 participants.

The pipeline remains inspectable at each transition. Source structure, exposure, conflict retention, classification, and human reading are measured as distinct layers rather than folded into a single output score.

## 02 / What the pipeline reveals

Explicit ranking raises source-structure amplification by **0.0047** over centrality-aware retrieval in the main controlled corpus. The structured outputs retain tuple-level source traceability while losing **0.9375** of encoded source conflict under the study's weighted measure.

In the automated-hiring anchor, official and competing material begins in a shared candidate environment, yet final external-source share reaches **0** for both Qwen3-4B and Gemma 3 4B. With selected sources held fixed, a conflict-retention policy lowers conflict loss by **0.364** for Qwen and **0.360** for Gemma. It also changes classification outcomes in the anchor, while category-boundary revision remains uneven across the controlled donor study.

<mark>Source diversity, conflict retention, and classificatory revision are separate properties. Improvement in one does not guarantee improvement in the others.</mark>

## 03 / Evaluation direction

The paper treats audit as a stage-by-stage inquiry:

- Which sources were available?
- Which sources survived selection?
- Which conflicts remained consequential during synthesis?
- Did a new distinction alter the final category or action route?

This produces an evaluation frame for AI-mediated interpretation that goes beyond output provenance alone. Traceability remains necessary; source composition, conflict retention, and classification must be visible alongside it.
